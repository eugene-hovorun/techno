Pad — two bars against a one-bar drum loop, so you can play a phrase in the first half and answer it in the second. Height is pitch in A minor pentatonic, so any line you draw fits.

Instrument — tap its icon for sound settings, S to solo from the next phrase, M to mute now, or » to mute from the next phrase.

Instrument icon — shape that layer: length and tone on the dial, stereo width and delay on the knobs, and a sweep that moves the tone on its own.

Chords — choose each slot from its dropdown. Every chord in the set is diatonic to A minor, so the line fits all four.

⚙ — tempo, volume, swing, delay, and tracks. Everything saves itself.

Tracks — while playing, a track comes in at the start of the next phrase; tap it again to cancel. Tap an empty slot under mine to keep what you have; tap save first to overwrite a slot, or restore to reload the selected factory preset.
